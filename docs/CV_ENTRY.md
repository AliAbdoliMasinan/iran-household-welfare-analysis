**Education, Income and Household Welfare in Iran** — Collaborative academic project  
Python, pandas, NumPy, statsmodels, scikit-learn, Matplotlib

- Implemented household survey preprocessing and OLS models with interaction terms to examine associations between household-head education, income, gender and urban-rural location.
- Constructed a PCA-based household welfare index combining regional-price-adjusted income and expenditure with consumption shares, and explored differences across provinces and survey years.
